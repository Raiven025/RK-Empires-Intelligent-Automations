The Challenge

Real estate businesses needed a centralized system to manage complex commission structures, distribute leads efficiently, and track broker performance. Everything was handled manually — commission splits calculated by hand, leads assigned ad hoc, and performance tracked across scattered spreadsheets. The processes were slow, error-prone, and impossible to scale.

The Solution

A centralized management platform built end-to-end and deployed to the cloud:

  • Automated commission engine — complex commission structures and splits calculated instantly, with no manual math
  • Lead distribution — incoming leads routed to the right brokers automatically
  • Broker performance tracking — activity and results visible in one place instead of scattered records
  • Automated email notifications — transactional updates delivered through Resend without human input

How It Was Built

Designed and developed with Claude AI and Windsurf, version-controlled on GitHub, and deployed on Railway for reliable cloud hosting. The result is a production system that runs around the clock — not a mock-up or a concept.

The Results

Hours → Instant Commission calculations that used to take the team hours now happen instantly
Fully Automated Lead distribution and email notifications run without human input
