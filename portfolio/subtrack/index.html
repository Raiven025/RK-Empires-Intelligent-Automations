SubTrack

A subscription intelligence platform that finds the recurring charges you forgot about — and puts the money back in your budget.

← Back to Portfolio
Claude AIWindsurfMicrosoft GraphRailwayAirtable

The Challenge

Businesses were losing thousands of dollars every month to forgotten subscriptions — tools nobody used anymore, duplicate services, and renewals that slipped through unnoticed. Manual tracking across inboxes and statements was completely inefficient and time-consuming, so it simply didn't happen.

The Solution

SubTrack, a subscription intelligence platform that does the tracking automatically:

  • Mailbox intelligence — connects through Microsoft Graph to detect subscription receipts and renewal notices automatically
  • Centralized subscription database — every recurring charge organized in Airtable with cost, owner, and renewal date
  • Savings identification — unused, duplicate, and overpriced subscriptions surfaced for review
  • Always on — deployed on Railway, monitoring continuously instead of waiting for a quarterly cleanup

How It Was Built

Designed and developed with Claude AI and Windsurf, integrated with Microsoft Graph for mailbox access and Airtable as the data layer, and deployed on Railway. A production system in daily use — not a mock-up or a concept.

The Results

$2,481/month In confirmed savings identified from forgotten and unused subscriptions
90% less time Subscription tracking effort reduced by nine-tenths — the platform does the work

“The subscription tracking platform Raiven built identified over $2,400 in monthly savings we didn't even know we had. The ROI was immediate. Professional, fast, and exactly what we needed.” — SaaS Manager, Tech Startup
